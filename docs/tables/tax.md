# Tax tables

Tax engagements run alongside (and downstream of) the regular member-program. A `client_tax_plans` row represents the engagement; specialists are attached via `client_tax_specialists`; per-task progress is tracked in `client_tax_progress`.

## `tax_planners` (added 2026-07-21)

As of 2026-07-22 a row also has a **portal login** (`tax_planner_logins`, the 5th portal / 6th login type — see [../architecture/04-auth-and-sessions.md](../architecture/04-auth-and-sessions.md)); every ADMIN surface remains service-role via the edge fn. RLS deny-all in the creating migration (`20260721100000_tax_planners.sql`; gotcha #141).

**The table holds TWO person-types, split by `planner_role` (2026-07-27), and since 2026-08-26 (v793) each has its OWN SLOT on a plan.** A **Team Member** logs into the SAME portal with the same capabilities, restrictions, allowlist and group-scope guards as a Tax Planner. They are the deliberate FIRST holder of a plan — but they hold it in `client_tax_plans.tax_team_member_id`, **never** in `tax_planner_id` (`allocate-planner.ts` 400s a role/slot mismatch in both directions). Their allocation raises the action-required "Allocate a Tax Planner for \<client\>" bell asking them to hand the plan on, and **they stay allocated alongside the planner afterwards**, keeping the plan's bells and their portal access. **The money question is now answered by the SLOT, not the role:** the whole planner payout leg keys off `tax_planner_id` alone, so a Team Member is never a payout destination — the 2026-08-10→2026-08-26 window in which they could be is closed. Gotcha #294 as amended twice. **FRONTEND, as of 2026-08-26:** the allocation STEP goes green once **either** slot is filled (allocating the team member is the admins' whole job on that step), but the `stepGate`'s `taxPlannerAllocated` / `allocDone` are **unchanged** and still require a real Tax Planner — so the *"Additional information required"*, *"Tax planner review complete"* and ROI-generation steps stay **LOCKED** with the hint *"Allocate a Tax Planner (not a Team Member) first"* until the hand-off happens (#390). **Portal write-rights on the allocation step are role-split:** a Team Member caller may add/remove either slot; a Tax Planner caller sees it **read-only** (chips, no `×`, no dropdown), fail-closed — an unresolved `self_role`, including the first paint before the roster loads, reads read-only.
