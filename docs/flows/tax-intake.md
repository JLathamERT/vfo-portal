# Tax intake — member-run tax client + $500 deposit (added 2026-09-17/18, v860–v864; feature-gated, resumable and Direct-aware since unit 2, 2026-09-18/21, v866–v873; card-or-ACH deposit since 2026-09-23, v886)

Unit 1 of [DIRECT to Tax Planning](../plans/direct-tax-planning/README.md), extended by unit 2. A member starts a **VFO Tax Planning** (program 4) client from the member portal instead of the disconnected Unbounce form: one 37-question form, a **$500 deposit paid by card or ACH** on the public **`/tax-deposit-pay` choice page** (a Stripe Checkout that has no customer — the client does not exist yet), and a server-side finalize that creates the enrollment, the client, the tax plan and the house invoice + receipt pair. The same form has three entry routes; two of them create a case, the third only records answers. Everything downstream of the created plan is the ordinary [tax-planning.md](tax-planning.md) track — this page stops where the Deposit Paid step is stamped, then picks up the two deposit money movements: the $250 team share, which the **"Tax planner review complete" Proceed** triggers since 2026-09-21, and the refund, which the **Tax Plan Red Light** step triggers.

| Route | Who fills the form | Who pays | Creates | Handler |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — member fills** | the member, in the portal | the member (choice page) | enrollment + client + plan + Deposit Paid | `tax_intake_submit` → `tax_intake_deposit_load` / `tax_intake_deposit_checkout` |
| **B — client link** | the client, on the public `/tax-intake?token=…` page | the client (choice page) | the same | `tax_intake_send_link` → `tax_intake_link_load` / `tax_intake_link_submit` → the choice page |
| **Holistic** | the member, for an EXISTING MAP 1 client | nobody — no deposit | nothing: answers stored against the client | `tax_intake_holistic_submit` |

On A and B the deposit is **waived** once the member has two qualifying clients (below). On the Holistic route there is never a deposit. A confirmed **VFO Tax Diagnostic** mints a fourth kind of row (`diagnostic_id` set, answers already stored) whose emailed **Pay deposit** button opens the same choice page — see [tax-diagnostic.md](tax-diagnostic.md).

## What a member sees

- **The VFO Tax Planning program is offered to every member whose `tax_intake` feature flag is on** — `MemberPortal.jsx` and `MemberMSMTracking.jsx` read `features.tax_intake` off `msm_load_enabled_programs` (`portal_feature_flags`, see the next section) and treat `'VFO Tax Planning'` (`TAX_PROGRAM_NAME`) as visible for that member regardless of `member_program_enabled`; while the flag is off, Tax Planning obeys the same `member_program_enabled` rule as every other program and the Add button is not rendered. With no program-4 `member_enrollments` row the Clients tab renders empty with the **+ Add new tax client** button; the first successful intake creates the enrollment server-side and the tab re-reads enrollments (`refreshEnrollments`).
- **+ Add new tax client** opens [TaxIntakeForm.jsx](../../src/components/member/TaxIntakeForm.jsx) in the Clients tab (`mode='form'`). Step 1 is two pill toggles (2026-09-18): first **who runs the case** — *VFO Services runs this case* (classic) or *I run this case (Direct)* — then **who fills the form** — *You answer the Tax Planning Form now* (route A) or *Send my client a link* (route B — first name, last name, email only). The Direct pill starts disabled and lights up only when `tax_intake_eligibility` answers `direct_eligible` (2+ qualifying clients AND the member's `tax_direct` flag); the note beneath it says why (*available once you have 2 qualifying tax clients — you have N* / *not available for your account yet*). Asked ahead of the fill choice on purpose — the two cards below advance the moment they are clicked, so a choice placed under them could never be made. The deposit line reads `Deposit: $500` or `Deposit: waived (you have N qualifying clients)` from `tax_intake_eligibility`.
- Route A ends on **Submit and pay deposit** (browser handed to the `/tax-deposit-pay` card-or-ACH choice page, below) or, when waived, **Submit** (the case is created inline). Route B ends on **Send link**. Both land on a *Thank you* card (`mode='done'`): *"Client created. We have emailed you a confirmation."* (card) / *"Bank transfer submitted. The client will be created once it clears (2-4 business days)."* (ACH, `&ach=1`) / *"We have drafted the Tax Planning Form link for <email>. It will be sent shortly."* / *"Thank you — your Tax Planning Form has been received."* (Holistic).
- **Return URLs.** A route A deposit Checkout returns to `/member?tab=msm_tax&intake=<id>&paid=1` (plus `&ach=1` when the member chose ACH); a cancel returns to the choice page (`/tax-deposit-pay?token=…&canceled=1`), not the portal. The Holistic email opens `/member?tab=msm_tax&intake_client=<clientId>`. `MemberPortal.jsx` lands either on the Tax Planning tab regardless of the last-used tab; `MemberClientsView` reads the params once, shows the done card (paid) or opens the form prefilled for that client (intake_client), re-reads the client list (again 4 s later, because the client is created by the webhook, which can land after the browser is back), and strips the params (`intake`, `paid`, `ach`, `intake_client`) from the URL with `replaceState`.
- **Test Member 59524 only:** a *Fill with test values* button on the member form and on the public page. The public page reads the flag from the token row (`test_member`, derived from `TEST_SANDBOX_MEMBER_NUMBERS`), never from the URL.

## Feature flag and the Direct choice (unit 2, 2026-09-18)

**`portal_feature_flags`** (migration `20260918200000`; `key` PK, `enabled_for_all`, `member_numbers text[]`, `note`, `updated_at`; RLS deny-all) carries two rows, `tax_intake` and `tax_direct`, both seeded **off** with allowlist `{59524}`. [utils/feature-flags.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/utils/feature-flags.ts) `featureEnabledForMember(sb, key, member)` is the only reader and **fails closed** — a missing row, a failed read or a thrown error is `false`, so a DB blip opens the feature to nobody. **Release = one SQL update (`enabled_for_all=true`), no deploy.**

What `tax_intake` gates, all on the member who owns the case: `tax_intake_submit` / `tax_intake_holistic_submit` / `tax_intake_send_link` / `tax_intake_link_submit` → 403 `This feature is not available for your account yet.` ahead of every write; `tax_intake_link_load` and the choice page's `tax_intake_deposit_load` / `tax_intake_deposit_checkout` → the same 404 a bad token gets (a public page tells a stranger nothing; a row minted by a VFO Tax Diagnostic rides the global `tax_diagnostic` flag instead — `depositFlagOpen` in `utils/tax-intake-deposit-checkout.ts`); the Holistic "complete the Tax Planning Form" email — `utils/tax-intake-request-email.ts` returns `{drafted:false, reason:"feature off"}` and does **not** stamp `clients.tax_intake_requested_at`, but the trigger is MAP 1 payment 1, which never recurs, so a Holistic client whose first payment lands while the flag is off is never asked automatically (accepted); and the member portal's Tax Planning tab + Add button (cosmetic — every write re-checks). `tax_intake_eligibility` deliberately answers `intake_enabled:false` rather than 403ing: it is the read the form renders from.

**The Direct choice.** `tax_route` on both writers (`tax_intake_submit`, and `tax_intake_send_link` — decided at SEND time, the client is never asked, refreshed on a re-send): absent/null = classic, `'direct'` = the member runs the case; anything else → 400 `tax_route invalid`; `'direct'` → 400 `Direct is not available for your account yet.` unless `directEligibleFor(count)` (`TAX_DIRECT_MIN_CLIENTS` is an alias of the waiver threshold, `utils/tax-direct-eligibility.ts`) AND the member's `tax_direct` flag, both **recomputed from the session's member number** — the browser asks, the server decides (#455). Stored on `tax_intake_requests.tax_route` (migration `20260918220000`) and carried by `finalizeTaxIntake` onto **`client_tax_plans.tax_route='direct'`**, **`clients.assigned_pf`** = the member's display name and **`clients.pf_member_number`** = the member (migration `20260918210000`) — the member IS the client's Planning Facilitator, which is what every PF email token (`resolvePfEmail`, #514) and every PF bell (`resolveTaxPf`, decision 28) resolve through afterwards. Everything downstream of that stamp is in [tax-planning.md](tax-planning.md).

## The form — one definition, mirrored

[utils/tax-intake-questions.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/utils/tax-intake-questions.ts) is the single server-side definition of the 37 questions (ids `q1`–`q37`), their order, options, `required` flags and the two validation message templates; [src/components/member/taxIntakeQuestions.js](../../src/components/member/taxIntakeQuestions.js) mirrors it **verbatim** (the array bodies are byte-identical bar the TypeScript annotation). The FE pre-validates from its copy and every handler re-validates with `validateTaxIntakeAnswers` + `normalizeTaxIntakeAnswers`; the two sides must move together.

- **Q1 "Who is completing this form?" is `type: "derived"`** — never rendered, never validated, and whatever the body sends is blanked. Route A and Holistic write `Advisor for Client` or `Accountant for Client` from the member's own record via `constants/member-types.ts isAccountantMember` (`member_category` first, the frontend's accountant `member_type` list as the fallback for rows whose category was never set); route B writes `Client`.
- **Q7–Q9** (Associated VFO Member / Introducer Email / Introducer Firm Name) are `hidden: true` — seeded from the member session (route A: name, email, `trading_name`; route B: the member's display name off the token row) and never shown.
- Q2/Q3 client first/last, Q4 email, Q5 phone, Q6 state (US states + DC + Canadian provinces), Q18 federal taxes paid — its first option is the *"Under $100k … not a good fit"* text (`TAX_INTAKE_Q18_POOR_FIT`), a legal answer that shows an inline warning but still submits.
- Answers are stored as jsonb on `tax_intake_requests.answers` and are **read-only afterwards** — nothing in the tax track pre-fills from them. Every surface renders them through the **Tax Planning Form** card (below).

## The deposit and the waiver

`TAX_INTAKE_DEPOSIT_CENTS = 50000` in [utils/tax-direct-eligibility.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/utils/tax-direct-eligibility.ts) — the price is fixed server-side; the browser never sends an amount and no body value can change it or who is charged.

**`qualifyingTaxClients(member_number)`** is the one predicate behind the waiver (and, in unit 2, Direct-route eligibility). A client qualifies when one of their `client_tax_plans` rows has `retainer_status='succeeded'` AND `post_review_client_decision` in `Proceed` / `Confirmed` / `Auto-Locked` AND `post_review_decision` in `Continue - Revenue Share` / `Undecided` AND neither `refund_status` nor `deposit_refund_status` is `succeeded` (the two "not refunded" filters run in JS because `.neq` drops NULLs, #28). **Distinct clients, not plans** — a second plan on the same person never buys a waiver. `depositWaivedFor(count)` is `count >= 2` (`TAX_DEPOSIT_WAIVER_MIN_CLIENTS`). **A lookup failure returns zero** — it charges rather than waives.

The waiver is **recomputed at every point it matters**, never read off a stored row: `tax_intake_eligibility` (what the form shows), `tax_intake_send_link` (the sentence in the client's email), `tax_intake_link_load` (the price the public form quotes), both submits (waived or not), and the choice page's `tax_intake_deposit_load` / `tax_intake_deposit_checkout` (the price actually charged). A member who crosses the line between send and payment is simply not charged.

On the choice page a row that has become waivable is moved `waived` by the checkout (guarded on a payable status and no ACH in flight) and finalized inline instead of being charged; the page shows *No deposit needed* with a **Continue** button for it.

A waived intake: `status='waived'` on insert, `deposit_required=false`, `deposit_amount_cents=0`, **no Checkout**, finalize runs inline, **no documents**, `[DEPOSIT_LINE]` empty in the confirmation, `deposit_payment_intent_id` NULL on the plan. The **Deposit Paid step is CLOSED as `N/A — No Deposit`** (finalize writes the `client_tax_progress` row with that status, 2026-09-18 / v864) — every downstream gate (Request Tax Returns, the Green/Red buttons, the Client Overview done-test in `utils/tax-plan-steps.ts`) keys on that row or on a PaymentIntent, so a waived plan with neither could never be advanced. The step renders the status as a chip with no `pi_` box. **Since 2026-09-21 the step formerly called Green/Red Light is the Stop-only "Tax Plan Red Light"**: it is shown on NO plan, waived or not, unless the reviewer said **Stop tax planning** (or it already carries history). On a waived plan that stop is **Stop tax planning** — `automation_TAX_stopnodeposit`, no money — rather than a refund. The team-share leg is stamped `N/A — No Deposit` at plan creation. See [tax-planning.md Step 0b](tax-planning.md#step-0b--tax-plan-greenred-light-decision-inside-tax-1---diagnostic).

## Route A — the member fills and pays

[actions/tax/intake-submit.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/actions/tax/intake-submit.ts) `tax_intake_submit` (AUTH, `MEMBER_SCOPED_ACTIONS`; 403 for any caller that is neither member nor admin; the member number is `auth.callerMemberNumber` for a member, `body.member_number` for an admin acting on a member's behalf — #455, the scoped-list rewrite is not itself a guard).

1. **403 unless the member's `tax_intake` flag is on** (2026-09-18); then `tax_route` shape (400 on anything but null/`'direct'`), then `qualifyingTaxClients` and the Direct eligibility test when `'direct'` was asked for (400) — refused on the route alone before the 37 answers are looked at.
2. Validate + normalise the answers (first error → 400 with `errors[]`); derive Q1. The count from step 1 → `waived`.
3. `loadSandboxConfigForMember(supabase, "TAX", member)` — member-keyed because no client exists; this is what forces 59524 into sandbox. Stamps `sandbox` and `stripe_account` (TAX is on `vfos`) on the row at mint time (#485).
4. Insert `tax_intake_requests` (`payer` defaults `'member'`, `status` `'waived'` or `'pending'`, `tax_route`, the client name/email/phone copied out of Q2–Q5, and — since 2026-09-23 — a fresh **`intake_token`** (`token32()`), the credential of the choice page only: the public `/tax-intake` form actions 404 a route A row by `payer`, so this token opens the payment page and nothing else).
5. **Waived:** `finalizeTaxIntake` inline → `{ waived:true, intake_id, client_id, tax_plan_id }`.
6. **Otherwise** no Checkout is minted here: the response is `{ waived:false, intake_id, url }` with `url` = `/tax-deposit-pay?token=<intake_token>` (the allowed browser origin, else `https://vfoportal.com`), and the form sends the browser there. The Checkout is minted by the choice page (next sections).

`lib/api.js` gives `tax_intake_submit` and `tax_intake_send_link` a 30 s timeout (the waived branch creates four rows and drafts a Gmail before answering); both are writes, so a timeout never auto-retries.

## Route B — the member sends the client a link

**Send.** [actions/tax/intake-send-link.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/actions/tax/intake-send-link.ts) `tax_intake_send_link` (AUTH, `MEMBER_SCOPED_ACTIONS`, same caller rule as route A). Requires first name, last name and a regex-valid email (400s), **the member's `tax_intake` flag (403 otherwise, ahead of the row and the email)**, and a valid `tax_route` (the Direct choice is made HERE — the client is never asked; eligibility recomputed server-side, 400 when refused). Computes the waiver and the member-keyed sandbox config, then **reuses any outstanding invitation** for the same member + same email (matched in JS, case-insensitive — not `.ilike`, because `_` is both a legal email character and a LIKE wildcard) where `payer='client'`, `status='invited'` and **`diagnostic_id` is NULL** (a row minted by a VFO Tax Diagnostic's Confirm is never reused, so a member cannot re-route a team-confirmed classic case to Direct): the row's name, deposit flag, `tax_route`, sandbox and `link_sent_at` are refreshed and **the same `intake_token` goes out again**, so a double click never leaves two live credentials for one person. Otherwise it inserts `payer='client'`, `status='invited'`, `intake_token=token32()` (32 random bytes as hex), `answers={}`. Then [utils/tax-intake-link-email.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/utils/tax-intake-link-email.ts) drafts template **277 `TAX_intake_link`** to the CLIENT with the green *Complete the Tax Planning Form* button onto `https://vfoportal.com/tax-intake?token=<intake_token>`; `[DEPOSIT_SENTENCE]` is the "A $500 deposit is taken at the end of the form…" sentence when the deposit applies at send time, empty when waived. The token is never echoed back to the caller. Response: `{ intake_id, email_drafted, deposit_required, client_name }`.

**Public page** [src/pages/TaxIntakePage.jsx](../../src/pages/TaxIntakePage.jsx) at `/tax-intake` (route in `App.jsx`), raw `fetch` like every other token page. Two `PUBLIC_HANDLERS` (no session) — **the `tax_intake_requests` row found by `intake_token` is the whole credential (#310)**: the body contributes the token and the answers and nothing else, so nothing in it can select a different row, member, Stripe account, price or recipient.

- [`tax_intake_link_load`](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/actions/tax/intake-link-load.ts): no/bad token → **404** *"This link is not valid."* — **and the identical 404 for a route A row** (`payer` not `client` and no `diagnostic_id`: its token opens the payment page only) **and when the INVITING member's `tax_intake` flag is off** (read off the token row, 2026-09-18; a diagnostic row reads `tax_diagnostic` instead); a `completed` / `paid` / `waived` row → `{ ok, status }` and nothing else (the page shows *already submitted*); a `pending` row with **`deposit_processing_at`** (an ACH debit in flight) → `{ ok, status:'processing' }` and nothing else (the page says the form is received and the bank transfer is on its way); a diagnostic row additionally returns `diagnostic:true` + `payer` and never the answers, and the page forwards it to `/tax-deposit-pay?token=…` (an old link already in an inbox); otherwise stamps `link_opened_at` once, recomputes the waiver, and returns the client's own name/email, the member's display name, `deposit_required`, `deposit_amount`, the question list and `test_member`. Never the member number, the intake id or any other client.
- [`tax_intake_link_submit`](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/actions/tax/intake-link-submit.ts): 404 on a bad token **or a route A row**; **403** when the inviting member's `tax_intake` flag is off (a diagnostic row: the `tax_diagnostic` flag); **409** *"This form has already been submitted."* unless the row is `invited`, `expired` (an abandoned Checkout, or a bank payment that failed and released the row), **or `pending` with no `stripe_payment_intent_id`, no `paid_at` and no `deposit_processing_at`** (2026-09-18 — the same abandonment, seen in the ~24 h before Stripe's `checkout.session.expired` lands; without that arm every resubmit 409'd for a day). On the pending and expired arms the handler first **expires the old Checkout session itself** (`expireStaleCheckout`, best-effort, with the key of the row's own `stripe_account` / `sandbox` stamp, #485) so it can never be paid against a row that has moved on — and if Stripe reports the old session already `complete`, money is in flight and the submit 409s. Validates the answers, writes `q1='Client'`, **strips CR/LF/tab and `<` `>` from q2/q3/q5** (they reach finalize's confirmation Subject and the PDFs), and **forces `q4` back to the invited email** — the client may correct their name and phone, but the address of record is half of what the token proved. A diagnostic row is pay-only: its **stored** answers are used and the body's are ignored. Recomputes the waiver and re-derives the sandbox config (the link may have sat in an inbox across a live/sandbox flip). Updates the row to `waived` / `pending`, guarded on `status IN (invited, expired, pending)` and `deposit_processing_at IS NULL` (a row that moved in between → 409). Waived → finalize inline. **Otherwise no Checkout is minted here**: the response carries `url` = `/tax-deposit-pay?token=<token>` and the form follows it to the choice page.

`TaxIntakePage` still checks `paid=1` **before** the status (a thank-you rather than *already submitted*) for a Checkout minted before the choice page existed; every current Checkout returns to the choice page instead.

## The choice page — `/tax-deposit-pay` (2026-09-23)

[src/pages/TaxDepositPayPage.jsx](../../src/pages/TaxDepositPayPage.jsx), public, raw `fetch`, built on the `/tax-pay` retainer page's two option cards and the Background Check request page's structure. **Every deposit lands here**: route A after the member's Submit, route B after the client's Submit, and a confirmed diagnostic's **Pay deposit** email button. Two `PUBLIC_HANDLERS`, and **the row found by `intake_token` is the whole credential (#310)**: the body carries the token (and the method) and nothing else.

- **`tax_intake_deposit_load`** ([actions/tax/intake-deposit-load.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/actions/tax/intake-deposit-load.ts)): bad token or flag off (`depositFlagOpen`) → 404. Returns `state` (`depositState`: `payable` for `pending`/`expired` or a diagnostic `invited` row; `processing` while `deposit_processing_at` is set; `paid` / `waived` / `completed`; `not_ready` for a route B row not yet filled in), the client's name, the member's display name, `payer`, `deposit_amount` 500, **`card_total` / `card_fee` computed server-side** (`cardChargeCents(500)` = **$515.24**, fee $15.24, from [constants/card-fee.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/constants/card-fee.ts) — the `/tax-pay` formula), a recomputed `deposit_required`, and `failed_before` (a released bank failure). Never the member number, intake id, answers or any email. Stamps `link_opened_at` once for a row a link was sent for.
- **`tax_intake_deposit_checkout`** ([actions/tax/intake-deposit-checkout.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/actions/tax/intake-deposit-checkout.ts)) — the ONE deposit Checkout builder. Body `{token, method}`; `method` not `card`/`ach` → 400 ahead of any lookup. 404 bad token / flag off; **409** unless `payable` (*"Your bank payment is already being processed."* while an ACH is in flight). Any previously stamped session is expired first with the row stamp's key: `complete` → 409 (money in flight), state **unknown** → 500 rather than leave two payable sessions. Recomputes the waiver (waivable → `waived` + finalize inline, `{waived:true}`), re-derives the member-keyed sandbox config, then mints `mode=payment`, **no customer**, `customer_email` = the payer's address of record (the member's `members.email`, or the invited client email), one line: **card** `payment_method_types=card` at `cardChargeCents(500)` = 51524 cents, or **ACH** `us_bank_account` at 50000 cents with **no `verification_method` pin** (#298 — bank sign-in first, manual entry as the fallback) and `custom_text[submit][message]` = `ACH_BANK_SIGN_IN_NOTE`. Memo on the line AND `payment_intent_data[description]`: `VFO Tax Planning Deposit - Client: <name> - Member: (<member#>) <member name>`. Metadata on the session **and** the PaymentIntent (#473): `pipeline=TAX`, `payment_kind=tax_intake_deposit`, `intake_id`, `member_number`, `deposit_method`. Success: route A → `/member?tab=msm_tax&intake=<id>&paid=1[&ach=1]`; route B and diagnostic → `/tax-deposit-pay?token=…&paid=1[&ach=1]`; cancel → `/tax-deposit-pay?token=…&canceled=1`. The session id, `status='pending'`, `stripe_account` and `sandbox` are stamped guarded on a payable status and no ACH in flight (a diagnostic `invited` row becomes `pending` here); a failed stamp expires the fresh session and answers 409/500. A Stripe error leaves the row untouched and payable.

The page shows ACH Bank Transfer (*No Fee*, $500.00) above Credit / Debit Card (*2.9% + $0.30 Fee*, $515.24, with the fee row), a notice after a cancel or a released bank failure, *Thank you — payment submitted* for an ACH in flight or an `ach=1` return, *Thank you* for a card return or a paid row, and *No deposit needed* + **Continue** when the member now qualifies for the waiver.

## Holistic route — an existing MAP 1 client

**Trigger:** [actions/pipeline/contract-invoice-receipt.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/actions/pipeline/contract-invoice-receipt.ts), after the invoice/receipt draft, `if (paymentNum === 1)` → `draftTaxIntakeRequestEmail(client_id)` in its own try/catch (nothing here can delay or break the money document). [utils/tax-intake-request-email.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/utils/tax-intake-request-email.ts) is **once-only via `clients.tax_intake_requested_at`** (written `.is(null)`, #327/#470 — this whole chain is redelivered on a Stripe retry) and also skips when a `tax_intake_requests` row already exists for the client. It drafts template **276 `TAX_intake_request|holistic`** to the MEMBER (needs a member email; Cc the assigned PF per the template) with the button onto `/member?tab=msm_tax&intake_client=<clientId>`; sandbox is the client's TAX config.

**Submit:** [actions/tax/intake-holistic-submit.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/actions/tax/intake-holistic-submit.ts) `tax_intake_holistic_submit` (AUTH, `MEMBER_SCOPED_ACTIONS`). `denyIfNotOwnClient(client_id)` runs **first**, before any other body field is read; then 403 for non-member/non-admin, 404 unknown client, **409** *"A Tax Planning Form has already been submitted for this client"* when any intake row carries that `client_id`. Inserts a row with `client_id` set, `deposit_required=false`, `deposit_amount_cents=0`, `status='completed'` and the derived Q1. **No deposit, no Checkout, no plan, no email, no enrollment** — the plan is started by an admin as before, and the answers card then shows on it.

## `tax_intake_requests` statuses

| Status | Meaning | Set by |
|---|---|---|
| `invited` | route B link drafted, form not yet submitted — or a diagnostic row confirmed and awaiting its deposit (payable on the choice page as it stands) | `tax_intake_send_link` / `tax_diagnostic_confirm` |
| `pending` | answers saved, deposit not yet paid. **An ACH in flight is still `pending`** with the side-column `deposit_processing_at` set (#475 — no new status value); such a row cannot mint another Checkout, and `tax_intake_link_load` answers it `status:'processing'` (a response value, never stored). ## Webhook — one metadata-keyed block ahead of every TAX branch

[router/webhooks.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/router/webhooks.ts) holds one block, placed after the Growth Credits fulfilment and **before the MAP 1 / TAX customer-keyed cascade**, **primary-account events only** (`fromPrimary` — TAX is on `vfos`), with a **positive guard on the object's own `metadata.payment_kind === 'tax_intake_deposit'`** (`isTaxIntakeDepositMetadata`, #473 — set on both the session and the PaymentIntent). The logic lives in [utils/tax-intake-deposit-webhook.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/utils/tax-intake-deposit-webhook.ts), the same shape as the Background Check request block. Every helper also refuses a row not stamped on the primary account or whose `sandbox` disagrees with `event.livemode` (#485), and every UPDATE carries its own status guard and `.select()`s what it moved, so a redelivery that matches nothing does nothing (#327). The session has no Stripe customer, so nothing customer-keyed below can claim it; the **two generic failure resolvers skip this kind** explicitly. The whole block is wrapped so a failure is logged and the webhook still answers 200.

- **`checkout.session.completed`** — the payer SUBMITTED; no `payment_status` gate (an ACH session completes `unpaid`). Row by `stripe_checkout_session_id`; fallback by `metadata.intake_id` only when that row has no session stamp or this one. A row already pointing at a DIFFERENT session → nothing booked and an action-required `FAILURE_tax_intake_deposit_ach` bell (*paid on a superseded Checkout*), unless that session's PI is dead (then ignored). `completed` → skipped. **`paid` → the resumable finalize is re-run** (a paid row whose finalize failed recovers on the redelivery; a healthy one no-ops on the #327 latch). Already booked on this PI → skipped. Otherwise the **PaymentIntent is fetched** (expanding `payment_method` + `latest_charge`) and its OWN status decides (#475):
  - **`succeeded` (card):** stamps `stripe_payment_intent_id`, `paid_at` = `existing || now` (#470), `status='paid'`, `deposit_payment_method_type`, `deposit_acct_last4`, `deposit_card_fee` (`amount_received − 500`, floored at 0), clears any earlier ACH-failure bell, then `finalizeTaxIntake`. **A finalize that returns `ok:false` or throws still answers 200 and raises the action-required `FAILURE_tax_intake_finalize`** (`notifyJakeFailure`, title `Tax intake finalize FAILED — intake <id>`, no rule row — Jake by code default); a success clears that title. An unreadable PI books from `session.payment_status` and raises a *details unreadable* bell under the same key.
  - **`processing` / `requires_action` (ACH):** booked **in flight** — `stripe_payment_intent_id`, method, last4, `deposit_processing_at`, and on `requires_action` `deposit_bank_verification_pending_at`; the row stays `pending` and **no case is created**. On `requires_action` the FYI bell **`TAX_intake_deposit_verification_pending`** (Jake + Tracy). For a bank payment the **ACH confirmation is drafted at submit** (`utils/tax-deposit-processing-email.ts`, once-only on `deposit_ach_email_sent_at`): `TAX_deposit_processing[|client]`, or its `|ach_verify` variant while verification is pending — the Tax retainer precedent (#287).
  - **anything else (a dead PI):** nothing booked; the session stamp is released (guarded) so the next mint does not 409 forever on a "complete" session.
- **`payment_intent.processing`** — only a row booked on this PI: clears `deposit_bank_verification_pending_at`. No email, except a **backstop** `TAX_deposit_processing[|client]` when the submit-time draft never landed.
- **`payment_intent.succeeded`** — the ACH **settled**: only a `pending` row booked on this PI with `deposit_processing_at` → `status='paid'`, `paid_at`, verification cleared, ACH-failure bell cleared, then the same finalize + bell. A card PI emits this too and is skipped (its row is already `paid`, no `deposit_processing_at`). **The case — and so the $250 team share — never precedes settlement.**
- **`payment_intent.payment_failed` / `payment_intent.canceled` / `checkout.session.async_payment_failed`** — **only a row BOOKED on that PI and in flight** (#482: Stripe cancels the PI of a page merely opened and abandoned; a card decline inside Checkout is retried there). Released: `status='expired'`, `deposit_failed_at`, PI / session / in-flight / verification / email-latch columns nulled, so the payer's original link works again; action-required **`FAILURE_tax_intake_deposit_ach`** (title `Tax intake deposit ACH FAILED — intake <id>`). Covers a bounce, a failure, and a micro-deposit verification that expires (~10 days, Stripe cancels).
- **`checkout.session.expired`**: `pending` → `expired` for that session id only, and only while no PI is booked.

## `finalizeTaxIntake` — one function, three callers, resumable

[utils/tax-intake-finalize.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/utils/tax-intake-finalize.ts) — called inline by the waived branch of both submits and of the choice page's checkout, by the webhook (a card at `checkout.session.completed`, an ACH at `payment_intent.succeeded`), and (2026-09-18) by the tax revshare sweep's **4th pass** ([actions/tax/revshare-sweep.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/actions/tax/revshare-sweep.ts): every `tax_intake_requests` row still `paid` with `paid_at` older than 10 minutes — the floor keeps it off a finalize still in flight — re-run, `clearJakeFailure` on success, reported as `intake_finalize_retried`), so none of them can drift.

**Resumable (2026-09-18, unit 2 phase 1).** The all-or-nothing latch was not enough: a run that died between the client insert and the plan insert stamped nothing, so the next run minted a SECOND client. Now it **re-reads the intake row fresh** before deciding anything (#427 — the caller's copy predates every write a previous run made; the webhook's freshly stamped PI is carried over if the re-read lacks it), **stamps `client_id` the instant the client exists and `tax_plan_id` the instant the plan does**, and a run that finds either stamp re-reads and REUSES that row (repairing a missing `client_enrollments` junction on the way) instead of creating another. A stamp that fails to land is itself a returned error — the next run would trust it. `created_client_at` is `existing || now` so a re-run never re-dates the case. The whole body is wrapped: it returns `{ok:false, error}` and never throws (both submits show the error, the webhook must answer 200).

1. **Latch (#327):** a row with `created_client_at` AND `client_id` returns `{ already:true }` with its existing ids and touches nothing.
2. **Enrollment:** find-or-create the member's program-4 `member_enrollments` row (`date_enrolled` today, `program_status='active'`, `training_status='pre'`). Any member may start a tax client, so the first intake is what enrolls them.
3. **Client:** if `intake.client_id` is stamped, re-read and reuse it; else `nextClientRef(sb, member, false)` → `clients` insert (`client_ref`, `enrollment_id`, `member_number`, name, email, phone, `status='active'`, **and on a DIRECT intake `assigned_pf` = the member's display name + `pf_member_number` = the member** — #514, the member is the PF), then stamp `client_id` on the intake.
4. **Junction:** `client_enrollments` (`client_id`, `enrollment_id`), selected first so a resumed run repairs a missing one rather than duplicating it — without it the client never appears in the member's list; a failure is logged loudly but does not undo the client.
5. **Plan:** if `intake.tax_plan_id` is stamped, re-read and reuse it; else `client_tax_plans` insert with `program_id=4`, `sandbox` = the intake's flag, `deposit_payment_intent_id` = the intake's PI (NULL when waived), **`tax_route`** (`'direct'` or NULL), and on a waived intake (no PI AND `deposit_required=false` — the PI wins the test) **`deposit_team_share_status='N/A — No Deposit'` from birth** (terminal, so no trigger or sweep can ever forward a share of money never collected); then stamp `tax_plan_id`.
6. **Deposit Paid step:** the program-4 task is found by its **sentinel `status_options='tax_deposit_pi'`** (task 114, never by id or name); the `client_tax_progress` row is inserted or updated to `Completed` when money moved, or to **`N/A — No Deposit` on a waived intake** (`deposit_required=false`, 2026-09-18), with `completed_date` today either way. A missing sentinel simply leaves the step for an admin.
7. **Row update:** `client_id`, `tax_plan_id`, `status='completed'`, `created_client_at=now`.
8. **Documents — only when money moved** (next section). Best-effort: the case exists and the money is in.
9. **Confirmation email** unless `confirmation_email_sent_at` is set (the narrower second latch — a finalize that crashed after the plan insert still sends exactly one email on the retry).

## The house invoice + receipt pair

[utils/tax-deposit-docs.ts](../../../vfo-edge-functions/supabase/functions/vfo-admin-api/utils/tax-deposit-docs.ts) `issueTaxDepositDocuments` mirrors the retainer pair in `actions/tax/invoice-receipt.ts`: the same `document_numbers` allocation, **`INV-<client_ref>-NNNN` (global sequence) / `REC-<client_ref>-NNNN` (per owner)**, the same html2pdf render, the same Drive folder **`<First> <Last> - <client_ref>`** under `GOOGLE_DRIVE_FOLDER_ID` (found or created), no vault copy. Everything falls back (today / a generic method with no digits) and never blocks the documents. Being built at finalize, an ACH deposit's documents exist only once it settles (#287: confirmation at submit, documents at settle).
- **Latch re-read fresh** off `tax_intake_requests`, not the caller's copy of the row. `deposit_docs_at` + both numbers present → **reuse arm**: the stored numbers, the two PDFs re-fetched from Drive.
- Numbers already stamped but no `deposit_docs_at` (a previous run reserved and then failed) → reused, not re-allocated. Freshly allocated numbers are **read back from `document_numbers` before either touches a document (#282)**; a missing reservation skips the documents and raises **`FAILURE_tax_deposit_docs`** (action-required, Jake by code default — there is no rule row; see Bells).
- Numbers are stamped on the row **before** the render; Drive ids + `deposit_docs_at` after the upload. A failed render returns null; a failed Drive token attaches without filing.

Returns null whenever no document could be produced, and the confirmation email then **drops its "attached" claim** rather than promising an attachment that is not there.

- **Tax Planning Form card** (`TaxIntakeCard`, above the phases, all three surfaces): one `tax_intake_load` per plan view; header shows *Completed by the member* or *Completed by the client via link on <date>* and *Submitted <date>*; expands to the 37 label/answer rows. Hidden when the client has no intake row — every pre-2026-09-17 plan and every case an admin started by hand.
- **Deposit Paid** (`tax_deposit_pi`): the `pi_` text input + Save (manual paste **kept**) on admin/planner, the saved PI as a chip on the member view, plus a green **Paid via portal** chip when the intake row's `tax_plan_id` is this plan. The two document numbers ride on the same `tax_intake_load` payload.
- **Tax Plan Red Light** (`tax_refund`, display name since 2026-09-21): **shown only when the reviewer said Stop**, or when the step already carries history (a legacy `Proceed`, a `Stopped`, a succeeded refund) — otherwise it is hidden and dropped from every count on every surface. When shown it is listed on all three surfaces; the member view is read-only (a red **Refunded $500.00**, a red **Stopped**, or a legacy `Proceed`) **except on a Direct plan**, where the member owns the one button. That button is **Refund** on a deposit plan and **Stop tax planning** on a waived one.
- **Tax planner review complete**: the **$250 team share chip** (`$250 team share paid` / `$250 team share failed` / `Awaiting Planner Allocation` / `N/A — No Deposit`) moved here on 2026-09-21, beside the Proceed that now fires the leg. **Admin-only** — VFO's own money movement, hidden from `readOnly` and `plannerMode` like the pricing split card.
- **Allocation chips on the member view:** `tax_load_plans` now carries `tax_planner_name` + `team_member_name` on each plan because the member view cannot load the planner roster (`tax_planners_load` is admin-only).

## Sandbox

Test Member 59524 is forced into sandbox by that helper on every route, the plan inherits `sandbox` from the intake, the confirmation and link emails redirect to the sandbox address off `intake.sandbox` / the global config, and the team-share and refund legs read the client's TAX config as every other tax money movement does. Route B's public page shows the test-values button only for an invitation whose token row belongs to a test member.
