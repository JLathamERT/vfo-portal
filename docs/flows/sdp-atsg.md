## What it is

A one-off track for the tax planning partner **SDP Personalized Planning**. SDP's own planners and team members add **their own clients** under one of our members and run them through:

1. **ATSG Advisory Retainer** — one yearly figure, billed annually (1 payment), quarterly (4) or monthly (12). The last payment of a year takes any cents left over. **It continues year after year on the same basis until stopped** (Jake 2026-11-20; it was one year, then done): see *Following years* below.
2. **Engagement agreement** — headed VFO Services alone ("the Firm" = VFO Services). The client signs in BoldSign, then **Anton Anderson countersigns for VFO Services** (`ATSG_CEO` in `shared.ts`, the same address as every other agreement; it was SDP's CEO until 2026-11-20), then the client is emailed the payment link.
3. **Payments** — the first on the client's link; every later one, this year's and the following years', charged automatically by the daily sweep on the saved card or bank.
4. **Strategy Impact Fees** — any number of one-off fees (amount, meeting date, strategy name), each with its own pay link. Locked until retainer payment 1 is paid.

**Entirely separate from our own clients by design (Jake):** its own tables (`sdp_atsg_*`), its own invoice / receipt numbering (`INV-` / `REC-<client_ref>-NNNN` on the payment row — never `document_numbers`), its own held shares (on the payment row — never `member_held_shares`; since 2026-11-19 each held leg is also noted in `payout_holds`, which is only a list, so it shows under Accounting > SDP ATSG > Held Payouts). Nothing here reads or writes `clients`, `client_tax_plans` or any program. Client refs are `<member>-SDP001`, `-SDP002`...

## Who can use it

| Caller | How | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| A **tax planner or team member** | the planner portal tab *SDP - Advanced Tax Strategy Governance*, shown when `sdp_atsg_access_load` says so | only when their group (`tax_planners.member_type` → `tax_planning_groups.name`) has `atsg_enabled`; only that group's cases (`loadCaseFor`) |
| An **admin** | More > **SDP ATSG**, granted per admin in the Admin Editor (tab key `sdp_atsg`, `TAB_ACTIONS.sdp_atsg`) | every case |
| The **client** | `/sdp-atsg-pay?token=` (PUBLIC, token only) | the token row's own case or fee |

Every handler calls `resolveAtsgCaller` (admin, or a planner of an `atsg_enabled` group, else 403) and `loadCaseFor` (a planner reaches only their group's cases) — the allowlists alone are not the fence (#257). The authed actions are in `ADMIN_ONLY_ACTIONS` (never a member's) and `TAX_PLANNER_ALLOWED_ACTIONS`. Live: `atsg_enabled` is on for SDP Personalized Planning only; on the test site every test group has it (`utils/staging-test-member.ts`).

**The planner switch `sdp_atsg_planners`** (More ▸ Feature Switches, *SDP ATSG for SDP planners*, Off / On only, 2026-11-17): while it is Off, `resolveAtsgCaller` refuses every planner and team member, so SDP sees no tab and cannot call any SDP action; admins are never affected. A missing row reads as Off. Released Off on live (Jake: SDP does not see it yet); On on the test site.

## The money

- Every payment lands on the **VFO Services** Stripe account (`STRIPE_ACCOUNT_PRIMARY`; sandbox per `atsgSandbox`).
- **Split:** VFO Services keeps **10%, locked** (back from 5% on 2026-11-20). SDP (the group's Connect account) and the member split the other 90%: SDP's % is typed (`sdp_pct`, 0 to 90 in steps of 0.5, default 45) and the member gets the rest (Jake, 2026-11-18). Year 1's is set with its amount and locked once the agreement is sent (on the case, copied onto every retainer row); a later year's can be changed under Following years; each Strategy Impact Fee has its own, locked once that fee is sent. All on the payment **before** any card fee (`splitAmounts`: VFO Services' 10% first, SDP its %, the member the rest, so a rounding cent is the member's). A 0% leg is marked `none_due`: no transfer, no revenue-share email.
- **The case screen's *Pricing & revenue split* card** shows each payee's share of the year's retainer and of all fees, and under it what has actually reached them so far and on how many of the payments ("Received $875.00 so far · 1 of 4 payments"), so one quarterly payout never reads as the whole year (Jake, 2026-11-18). Each sent fee's row shows its own split. The card fee (2.9% + $0.30 grossed up, `cardChargeCentsFor`) stays with VFO Services. ACH has no fee. The split is stamped on the row at the payout (`sdp_share` / `member_share` / `vfos_share`, #659).
- Transfers carry `source_transaction` (the client's charge); Stripe refuses a `transfer_group` beside it when the charge already has one, so `transfer_group` is set only when there is no charge to tie to (#759).
- **The member's share is held, never paid,** when the member is suspended / paused / in arrears (`held_suspended` / `held_paused` / `held_arrears`, with the usual held notice), or has no working payout account (`awaiting_connect`: the usual payout-setup request email, unless one already went, plus Jake's bell). The daily sweep pays it once the hold clears. SDP's share is held the same way if SDP's account stops working.
- **Accounting > SDP ATSG** (2026-11-19, admins with the Accounting tab; the SDP ATSG tab itself is unchanged): **SDP ATSG Revenue** lists every cleared payment with its SDP / member / VFO Services split and a note on any share not yet paid (`sdp_atsg_revenue_load`, read-only; a row opens the case); **Held Payouts** lists every held SDP or member share with its reason and a **Retry now** button, which runs `automation_SDP_ATSG_settle` for that payment (`held_payouts_load` / `held_payout_retry`, engine `sdp_atsg`, [../architecture/07-server-chains.md](../architecture/07-server-chains.md) *Held Payouts*).

## Step by step

| Step | Action / trigger | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Add client | `sdp_atsg_case_create` | A case row, status `new`. Member picked from `sdp_atsg_members_load`, which also lists everyone at the group for the case's **Cc on client emails** ticks (a planner adding it starts ticked; an admin's case starts with nobody). |
| Cc list | `sdp_atsg_save_cc` | The case's *Cc on client emails* card: tick or untick anyone at the group, any time; the next email onward uses it. |
| Retainer | `sdp_atsg_save_retainer` | `retainer_amount` + `frequency` + SDP's % of the 90% (`sdp_pct`); year 1's rows (`year` 1); editable until the agreement is sent. |
| Preview | `sdp_atsg_agreement_preview` | The PDF exactly as it would be sent. |
| Send agreement | `sdp_atsg_send_agreement` (claims `agreement_sent_at` first) | Renders `agreement_templates` pipeline `SDP_ATSG` with html2pdf (Letter, margin 0, #535), sends through BoldSign with the client first and the CEO second (EnableSigningOrder), emails `SDP_ATSG_agreementsent` with the embedded signing link. BoldSign sends no emails of its own. Status `agreement_sent`. |
| Client signs | BoldSign `Signed` → `boldsign-webhook` → `automation_SDP_ATSG_boldsign_event` | Status `client_signed`; `SDP_ATSG_ceocountersign` to the CEO with his embedded link; bell. |
| CEO signs | BoldSign `Completed` → same | Status `awaiting_payment`; Stripe customer + the case's durable `pay_token`; the retainer rows; `SDP_ATSG_paymentemail`; bell. |
| Declined / Expired / Revoked | BoldSign event → same | The case goes back to `new` (agreement fields cleared) with `agreement_declined_at` + `agreement_declined_event`; the case screen shows the event as a chip and a notice above the pricing form; bell. |
| First payment | `/sdp-atsg-pay` → `sdp_atsg_pay_checkout` (Stripe Checkout, card or ACH) → Stripe webhook | Card: row `paid` at once. ACH: `processing` + `SDP_ATSG_ach_confirmation`, `paid` when it clears. A quarterly / monthly retainer saves the method and sets the year's due dates. Status `active`. |
| Settle (every paid row) | `automation_SDP_ATSG_settle` (chained in the background) | Invoice + receipt PDFs (one invoice per retainer YEAR, its number on that year's first row, re-issued with each of the year's payments; a fee has its own), saved to Drive `SDP ATSG/<client - ref>/`, emailed in `SDP_ATSG_invoicereceipt`; the two transfers; the two revenue-share emails (`SDP_ATSG_revshare|sdp`, `SDP_ATSG_revshare|member`, totals for that year). |
| Later payments | `automation_SDP_ATSG_sweep`, daily 13:20 UTC (`sdp-atsg-sweep-daily`) | First the renewal pass (below). Then charges every due retainer row, of any year, off-session on the saved method. A decline: the row goes `missed`, `SDP_ATSG_payment_failed` to the client (once per row), Jake's bell. Missed rows are collected again alongside the next row that falls due, oldest first, stopping at the first refusal. Then retries anything paid but not settled (10+ minutes after `paid_at`). |
| Heads-up | the sweep, 30 days before the client is emailed next year's terms (≈ month 10) | `SDP_ATSG_renewal_heads_up` bell to Jake and the SDP people ticked on the case (while the planner switch is On; their bell opens `/tax-planner?tab=sdp_atsg&case=N`), once per year (`renewal_heads_up_year`). The case screen shows an amber notice with *Open Following years* until the client email date. |
| Next year set up | the sweep, 11 months into the latest year (`reminder_on`) | Writes the next year's rows (`year` + 1, `year_seq` 1..N, `seq` keeps counting) on the case's terms, or on a change saved for next year (`next_retainer_amount` / `next_frequency` / `next_sdp_pct`, then cleared), due from the anniversary of payment 1; the case's `retainer_amount` / `frequency` / `sdp_pct` become that year's. Then `SDP_ATSG_renewal_reminder` to the client (retainer continues, nothing to do, reply for a meeting, update-method button), claimed on `renewal_reminder_year` and released if it fails, + the `SDP_ATSG_renewal_reminder` bell. |
| Following years | `sdp_atsg_save_next_retainer` (the case's Following years card) | Amount, billing and SDP's % for the next year. Not yet set up: saved on the case (`next_*`) and the reminder carries it. Set up but not started: that year's rows are rewritten at once, and if the client was already reminded and the amount or billing changed, `SDP_ATSG_retainer_changed` goes to them. Refused while the switch below is off. |
| Continue next year | `sdp_atsg_set_renewal` (the switch on the Following years card) | Off (`renewal_off`): no next year, no heads-up, no reminder; a next year already set up but not started is removed (its terms kept as next year's). The year under way is still collected; on its anniversary, once every row is paid or canceled, the sweep marks the case `completed`. If the client had already been reminded, nothing is emailed: SDP tells them. On: back to continuing (the next sweep sets the year up if its date has passed, with no second reminder for a year already announced). |
| Pay what was missed | the failure email's link (the case `pay_token`) | Collects every missed row in one Checkout and saves the new method. The page shows an amber notice naming the missed payments and their due dates, and "- missed" on each line. |
| Update payment method | `sdp_atsg_send_update_link` → `SDP_ATSG_update_method` → `/sdp-atsg-pay?token=…&update=1` | Stripe Checkout in setup mode: saves the new card or bank, charges nothing. Open any time a method is on file and the case is not stopped (2026-11-20: the page no longer closes once a year's rows are all paid). Every retainer payment saves its method now, annual included. |
| Strategy Impact Fee | `sdp_atsg_impact_fee_create` | A `requested` row with its own `pay_token` and its own `sdp_pct` (default 45); `SDP_ATSG_impactfee_request` to the client. The strategy name joins `sdp_atsg_strategies` for good. No Resend or Cancel (Jake). |
| Stop | `sdp_atsg_stop` (the Live / Stopped switch; one way) | Every scheduled / requested / missed / declined row `canceled`; the case `stopped`; its pay links close; the sweep skips it. Payments already made stay. |
| Payout setup | `sdp_atsg_member_payout_request` | The usual payout-setup request, for this case's member only (#490). |

## Statuses

- `sdp_atsg_cases.status`: `new` → `agreement_sent` → `client_signed` → `awaiting_payment` → `active`, which runs year after year; `completed` only when *Continue next year* was turned off and the last year has ended and been paid; or `stopped` from any point.
- `sdp_atsg_payments.status`: `scheduled` | `requested` (an impact fee) | `processing` (ACH) | `paid` | `missed` (a later retainer row that failed) | `declined` (a first payment or fee that failed) | `canceled`.
- Payout statuses: `paid` | `none_due` (a 0% leg) | `awaiting_connect` | `held_suspended` | `held_paused` | `held_arrears` | `failed` (retried by the sweep with a fresh idempotency key, #489).

## Emails and bells

- **12 emails**, pipeline `SDP_ATSG`, all Send mode, in their own section of the Email Templates tab. Since 2026-11-20 the client subjects say *VFO Services* (not *SDP & VFO Services*), the agreement email thanks the client for choosing VFO Services, and two are new: `SDP_ATSG_renewal_reminder` and `SDP_ATSG_retainer_changed`. The invoice, receipt and pay page are from VFO Services alone. Client emails go To the client, Cc the member and the people ticked on the case (`sdp_atsg_cases.sdp_cc_emails`). Only the group's **active planners and team members, as individuals,** can be ticked (`loadSdpPeople`; never the group's contact address, Jake 2026-11-17), the server keeps only names on that list (`pickSdpCc`), and someone who leaves the group stops being Cc'd at the next send. The group contact still gets SDP's revenue-share emails. Each subject ends with the person's name (`withSubjectName`, #679). "SDP Personalized Planning" is fixed text, never a placeholder.
- **9 bells**, in their own colour (fuchsia `#c026d3`): client signed, countersigned, agreement declined / expired / revoked, payment received, payment failed, transfer failed, member share held, renewal reminder sent (or failed) — Jake only — and the 30-day heads-up, to Jake and the ticked SDP people. A failure / held / transfer bell's title ends with the payment's label, so a second event on the same case is not de-duplicated away while the first is unread (#758).

## Testing it on the test site

- **To test a later year**, move the case's start back: `update sdp_atsg_payments set due_date = '<date>' where case_id = N and seq = 1`. Year Y+1 is set up (and reminded) when today ≥ that date + 12·Y − 1 months; the heads-up comes 30 days earlier; a later year's first payment is charged when today ≥ that date + 12·Y months. Payment rows already written keep their own dates.
- Run the sweep exactly as the job does: `do $$ begin execute (select command from cron.job where jobname='sdp-atsg-sweep-daily'); end $$;` — the request leaves only when that statement commits, so read `net._http_response` in a LATER call (#760).
- A card that saves but declines every charge: Stripe's `4000 0000 0000 0341`, entered through the update-payment-method link.
- Agreement Declined / Expired / Revoked only arrive if the BoldSign webhook subscribes to them (#757).
- The test site's reset clears SDP data with the rest: a test team's reset deletes its group's cases (`clearStagingTeamWork`), a test member's reset its cases (`MEMBER_KEYED_LOOSE`), and the full wipe all three tables (`STAGING_WIPE_TABLES`).
