Mirrors Specialists (`experts`): admin CRUD + document vault + certifications. Lives in the "Tax Planners" 5th key admin section (2026-07-21). | **Team Member** (tax) | The second `tax_planners` person-type, distinguished by `tax_planners.planner_role = 'Team Member'` (2026-07-27). Gotcha #294. | **FRONTEND EXCEPTION as of 2026-08-13 (#390):** the Tax Priorities `stepGate` computes `taxPlannerAllocated` as `planner_role === 'Tax Planner'`, so while a **Team Member** holds the plan the *"Additional information required"* and *"Tax planner review complete"* steps stay **LOCKED** with the hint *"Allocate a Tax Planner (not a Team Member) first"*. **Backend capabilities are unchanged** — the allowlist, group-scope guards and every handler still treat the two roles identically. Mirrors `strategic_member_groups`: the group NAME is a `tax_planners.member_type` option, a RENAME cascades planners' `member_type`, and the tax payout engine resolves the transfer destination `tax_planners.member_type` → `tax_planning_groups.name` (exact match) → `stripe_account_id`. Managed under Tax Planners → Tax Planning Partners. (2026-07-21) |
| **Tax Planner Share** | The third leg of the 3-way Tax Planning revenue split (Member / Tax Planner / VFOS), stored as `client_tax_plans.tax_planner_share` (a dollar amount of the TOTAL engagement). Paid proportionally per installment (`share/total × payment`) by `utils/tax-planner-payout.ts` to the planner's **Tax Planning Group** Connect account. Preset `1/3 Member, 1/3 Tax Planner, 1/3 VFOS` + Custom on `TaxDecisionForm`/`TaxPricingForm`; strategic variants in `src/lib/strategicSplits.js` (`programType='tax'`). (2026-07-21) |
| **Initial retainer** | On a revised-process tax plan of **$31,000 or more**, the first of the two retainer payments — a flat **$15,000** for every client except one on the **custom-split allowlist** (see below). Stored as `client_tax_plans.initial_retainer_amount`; NULL on legacy plans and on a plain `≤ $30,000` 2-payment plan, but **NOT** NULL on a **buffer-band** plan or a **converted** one, which both keep it as the reversibility marker — which is why `isThreePaymentPlan` keys on `final_retainer_amount` instead (**#440**). Its own card fee lives on `card_processing_fee`, which since 2026-08-26 describes THIS payment and no other (**#450**). See [flows/tax-fee-process.md](flows/tax-fee-process.md). (2026-08-26) |
| **Final retainer** | The second retainer payment on such a plan — `retainer_amount − initial_retainer_amount`, i.e. whatever remains of the 50% retainer once the initial is subtracted (usually `− 15000`, but see **Custom split**). Stored as `client_tax_plans.final_retainer_amount`, with its own card fee on `final_retainer_card_fee` since 2026-08-26. Collected **off-session** when the client clicks the green Client decision 1 button, and its settlement is what releases the retainer revenue share. **`isThreePaymentPlan` is keyed on THIS column, not on `initial_retainer_amount`** (gotcha **#440**). See [flows/tax-fee-process.md](flows/tax-fee-process.md). (2026-08-25) |
| **Fee process version** | `client_tax_plans.fee_process_version`. **NULL = the legacy fee process** (untouched forever); **`'2026-08-25'` = the revised process** (one total in, every amount derived server-side). Stamped only while `retainer_status IS NULL`. See [flows/tax-fee-process.md](flows/tax-fee-process.md). (2026-08-25) |
| **Buffer band (tax fee)** | The total range **$30,000.01 – $30,999.99**, added 2026-09-01. A total inside it is a **2-payment** plan whose retainer is the flat **$15,000** (implementation = `total − 15,000`) rather than a 3-payment plan with an absurdly small final retainer — `$30,119` used to give `$59.50`. Decided in `splitFeeCents()`, and **stored exactly like a converted plan** (`retainer_amount = initial_retainer_amount = 15000`, `final_retainer_amount` NULL), so every surface reads it as 2-payment with no code change and a later Tax 4 amendment reaching `$31,000` converts it up. The band's width is what makes **$500** the smallest final retainer that can exist. A custom split is refused on it. See [flows/tax-fee-process.md](flows/tax-fee-process.md). (2026-09-01) |
| **Conversion (tax fee)** | A Tax 4 amendment to a total **below $31,000** — at or under $30,000, or inside the **buffer band** — that turns a 3-payment plan back into a 2-payment one by nulling `final_retainer_amount` while KEEPING `initial_retainer_amount` as the reversibility marker. Reversible by a later amendment reaching $31,000, until Client decision 1 is sent. The same arm is what a buffer-band plan's stored shape reproduces. See [flows/tax-fee-process.md](flows/tax-fee-process.md). (2026-08-25, band 2026-09-01) |
| **Custom split** (tax payments) | The **"Allow custom split"** toggle on the Tax 3 decision form, which lets an admin type an **initial retainer** other than the flat $15,000 on a 3-payment plan. Gated to `CUSTOM_SPLIT_CLIENT_IDS` in `constants/tax-fee-process.ts` (client 105 = Chris Colby, the engagement it was built for; client 62 = the sandbox Test Client, kept so the path stays testable) and enforced backend **and** frontend. It moves only the initial/final boundary INSIDE the retainer half — the 50:50 retainer/implementation split of the total is unaffected, and every downstream surface reads the stored columns, so nothing else changed. **Do not confuse with the revenue `splitType = 'Custom'`** on the same form, which divides the fee between Member / Tax Planner / VFOS: two unrelated concepts that share a word. See [flows/tax-fee-process.md](flows/tax-fee-process.md). (2026-08-26) |
| **Card-update / payment-method change** | The admin-initiated flow ("Phase D", 2026-06-16) that lets a client/member/specialist swap the card or bank account on a reusable engagement without a re-charge. | Jake (`is_superadmin`) clicks **"Send Email to Change Payment Method"** in a person's Payments tab (`PaymentsHeader` → `payments_send_card_update`), which drafts the secure update-link email (`email_templates` `pipeline='PAYMENTS'`, `template_name='card_update'`). The recipient lands on the public `/update-card` token page ([UpdateCardPage](src/pages/UpdateCardPage.jsx)), picks an engagement, and enters new card/bank details on Stripe in **setup mode** (no charge). Admin-only — never exposed in the client/member/specialist self-service portals. |

## Technical terms

| Term | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Edge function** | A Deno-based serverless function deployed to Supabase. Two of them in this system. |
| **Error boundary** | A React class component that catches uncaught render/runtime errors in its child tree (`getDerivedStateFromError` + `componentDidCatch`) and renders a fallback UI instead of letting the whole app crash to a blank screen. The app-wide one is [`src/components/ErrorBoundary.jsx`](src/components/ErrorBoundary.jsx), mounted in `src/main.jsx` (2026-06-18). |
| **Sentry** | Third-party frontend error-monitoring service (`@sentry/react`). Initialized in `src/main.jsx`; the error boundary forwards crashes via `Sentry.captureException`. Error monitoring only — no Session Replay, no performance tracing. The DSN is a public ingest-only key hardcoded in source (not a secret). See [integrations/sentry.md](integrations/sentry.md). |
| **Rate limiting / 429** | Server-side throttle on repeated login attempts (security item H1). After too many tries the login action returns HTTP `429` "Too many login attempts"; the frontend surfaces it inline on the login form (see the `callApi` login-401/429 handling in [architecture/02-frontend-shell.md](architecture/02-frontend-shell.md#login-error-handling-in-callapi-2026-06-18)). |
| **IDOR** | Insecure Direct Object Reference — an access-control bug where a caller reaches another user's record by supplying its id, because the server scopes the query off the request body instead of the session. The deny-by-default role gates + session-derived scoping (e.g. `auth.callerClientId` / `callerSpecialistId`, never the body) in [architecture/04-auth-and-sessions.md](architecture/04-auth-and-sessions.md) are the defenses against it. |
| **RLS** | Row-Level Security in Postgres. Enabled on all public tables; bypassed by service-role key. |
| **Anon key** | Public Supabase JWT used as a default `Authorization: Bearer` header by the frontend. Hardcoded in [src/lib/api.js](src/lib/api.js). |
| **Service-role key** | Privileged Supabase JWT used by edge functions. Bypasses RLS. Never exposed to frontend. |
| **Sandbox mode** | Per-pipeline toggle on `pipeline_sandbox_config`. When `true`, automation emails route to `sandbox_email`, and Stripe/BoldSign use sandbox keys. |
| **`vfos` / `ert`** (Stripe account) | The two Stripe accounts the portal bills on since **2026-09-11**. `vfos` = **VFO Services**, the original and still the default — Tax, MAP 1, PIP, SpecRev, the specialist background check + $99 licence, **every Connect account / transfer**, `/update-card`, and the whole Payment Continuation family. `ert` = **ERT** (Elite Resource Team LLC) — member membership fees, Growth Credit purchases, and advisor/accountant onboarding money. Two rules answer two questions: `pipeline_sandbox_config.stripe_account` decides where a **NEW** customer is minted; the row's own nullable `stripe_account` (**NULL = legacy = `vfos`**) governs every later call on that row. All 37 membership plans were moved to `ert` on 2026-09-11 by `membership_stripe_remap`. See [integrations/stripe.md](integrations/stripe.md) and gotcha **#485**. |
| **Reminder ladder / stall ladder** | The two-tier chase a daily sweep runs against a stalled step: tier 1 re-emails the person who hasn't acted (guard column `*_reminder_sent_at`), tier 2 raises an escalation bell to the PF / Team Member / Tracy (guard `*_pf_notified_at`). Defaults are **2 and 4 business days**, per-rule editable as `notification_rules.delay_days`. A third, purely MANUAL tier exists — the `<stall>_pf_ack_at` "Reached out?" tick (gotcha #381) — which no sweep reads. Ladders live in `actions/*/sweep.ts`; every tier is a separate PostgREST query, so a stall with no guard column cannot be laddered. |
| **Business day (sweep delays)** | Mon–Fri **UTC**, with **no holiday calendar** — a US federal holiday still counts as a working day. Since 2026-08-14 every sweep delay is measured this way by `utils/notify.ts businessDelayCutoffIso(days)` (backward weekday walk from `now`; the fractional part the Notification Editor allows, `step=0.5`, is subtracted as hours only AFTER the walk so a larger `delay_days` is always an earlier cutoff). `businessDayHorizonDateOnly(fromDateOnly, days)` is the forward mirror, used by the only two **lookahead** windows: the Tax 2 assess-form countdown before `tax3_meeting_date` and the check-client pre-due-date nudge. The stored `delay_days` numbers did NOT change — only the unit — and bells now read "N business day(s) have passed". The predecessor `delayCutoffIso` is still exported but has **zero callers**. Deliberately still on the CALENDAR: the advisor/accountant 14-day auto-decline, the Tax 4 "meeting has passed" nudge, the membership 30-day renewal notice, the chargescheduled sweep (charges land on real due dates, weekends included), the notifications purge, personal reminders, and all token/session expiry windows. |
| **`'Auto-Locked'`** | A **historical-only** value on `client_tax_plans.implementation_final_decision` and `post_review_client_decision`. It meant "the client stayed silent past the grace window, so the sweep decided for them". **Nothing writes it any more** — Tax 4 Continue has been click-only since gotcha #264, and the Tax 5 implementation auto-charge tier was deleted 2026-08-14, leaving `automation_TAX_charge_implementation` reachable from exactly one place, the client's own Proceed click in `actions/tax/implement-final-decision.ts`. `actions/clients/overview-tax.ts` still READS the value so pre-existing rows keep rendering as "continue"; treat it as read-only vocabulary and never reintroduce a writer. |
| **Embedded sign link** | A BoldSign URL that opens in an iframe (vs an emailed BoldSign-hosted link). Used so the client / CEO sign within VFO's own email layout. |
| **Server-to-server chain** | One automation handler invoking another via `fetch` against the same edge function URL with `Authorization: Bearer <SERVICE_ROLE_KEY>`. Routes to public-token actions, bypassing the user-session gate. |
| **Token-link page** | A frontend page (`/decide`, `/pay`) that takes a token in the URL query string and uses raw `fetch` (no session) to call public actions on the edge function. |
| **html2pdf.app** | Third-party HTML→PDF generation service. Called from `automation_CONTRACT_invoicereceipt` and `automation_CONTRACT_sendagreement`. |
| **Stripe Connect** | Stripe's marketplace feature where the platform makes Transfers to connected merchant accounts. Used for revenue share payouts to members. Each member's account ID is in `members.stripe_account_id`. |
| **Setup mode** | A Stripe Checkout Session created with `mode:'setup'` — it collects and saves a payment method (card or bank) for future off-session use **without charging anything**. Used by the card-update / payment-method-change flow (`payments_cardupdate_checkout` → `/update-card`) so a client/member/specialist can swap their card or bank on a reusable engagement. Contrast with the `mode:'payment'` (one-time charge) and `mode:'subscription'` (recurring, e.g. the $99/mo specialist license) sessions used elsewhere. |
| **gh-pages** | GitHub Pages static hosting. The deploy target for `vfo-react`. Served at the domain root `vfoportal.com` (no path prefix). |

## Cross-references

**Step gate (tax track)** — the `stepGate(task, phase)` prerequisite lock in `TaxPrioritiesTab.jsx` (2026-08-13). A step whose **direct** prerequisites are unmet renders an inert row — a red circle-slash icon plus a gray hint naming the blocking step — instead of its control. Earlier gates make the chain transitively safe without recursion. Escape hatches are deliberate: the Green/Red Light row is never locked (only its *Proceed* button is), `reviewStop` unlocks the ROI-booked step on Holistic only, and `tax6Started` keeps historic Tax 6 work editable. Steps already answered are always exempt, and the member portal is exempt entirely. **No backend enforcement** — `tax_save_task` still accepts a write to a locked step. See flows/tax-planning.md and gotchas #390–#392.
